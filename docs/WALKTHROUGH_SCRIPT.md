> **Target Audience:** Developers, AI engineers, knowledge workers, and open-source contributors.  
> **Duration:** ~3 Minutes  
> **Format:** Screen capture with voiceover.

---

## Act 1: The Linear Problem & The Branching Spark (0:00 – 0:40)

**[Visual: Open standard linear chat UI vs GraphMind]**

> **Voiceover:**  
> *"Traditional AI chats have a fatal flaw: human thought isn't linear. The moment you ask a follow-up question or explore a tangent, your original context is buried forever in an endless scrolling text wall.*

**[Visual: Type a prompt about Python Concurrency, highlight "Event Loop", and click "Explore Branch"]**

> **Voiceover:**
