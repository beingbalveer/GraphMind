# ADR-0004: Selection of Apache License 2.0

- **Status:** Accepted (Frozen)
- **Date:** 2026-08-17
- **Deciders:** Founding Team / Architect

---

## Context and Problem Statement

Choosing the right open-source license is critical for community trust, developer adoption, enterprise friendliness, and contributor governance.

---

## Decision Drivers

- Encourage open adoption by individual developers and enterprise teams.
- Provide explicit patent protection and license grants.
- Establish GraphMind as a community-first open-source project.

---

## Considered Options

1. **MIT License**: Extremely permissive, brief, but lacks explicit patent rights protection.
2. **Apache License 2.0**: Permissive, widely understood, includes explicit patent grants and protection against patent trolling.
3. **AGPL-3.0**: Copyleft license requiring all derivative network services to open-source their code (can deter commercial/enterprise adoption).

---

## Decision Outcome

**Chosen Option: Apache License 2.0.**

### Rationale:
- Strikes the optimal balance between open developer contribution and enterprise confidence.
- Standard choice for major AI and infrastructure projects (e.g., Kubernetes, TensorFlow).

---

## Consequences

### Positive:
- High community trust and enterprise adoption potential.
- Explicit patent protection for contributors and users.
