## 3. Automated accessibility evidence (axe-core)

New helper `apps/web/src/test/a11y.ts` exports `expectNoA11yViolations(container)`. It
runs `axe.run` and fails on any violation. Two rule families are disabled by design
and covered by the manual checklist (§6):

1. `color-contrast` — jsdom cannot compute real layout/paint.
2. Document-level rules (`bypass`, `document-title`, `html-has-lang`, `region`, and
   the `landmark-*` / `page-has-heading-one` set) — these describe a complete HTML
   document with the app shell; the scenes render isolated components/fragments.

All other rules (names, roles, labels, aria wiring, duplicate ids, form labels, list
structure, etc.) run at full strength.

Two new test files render the named surfaces and assert zero violations:

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 3.1 Automated finding fixed

axe flagged `label` on the files/library surface: the visually hidden
`<input type="file">` in `FileLibraryView.tsx` had no accessible name (the visible
"Upload" button merely clicks it). Fixed by adding `aria-label="Upload files"` and
`tabIndex={-1}` to the hidden input.

**Actual Result:** the library scene went from `1 violation` to `0`; the new test
files pass.

### 3.2 Reduced motion (automated basis)

Reduced motion is enforced globally, so per-component `motion-reduce:` guards are
reinforcement rather than the only mechanism:

* `src/app/globals.css` `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` neutralizes
  `animation-duration`, `animation-iteration-count`, `transition-duration`, and
  `scroll-behavior` for all elements.
* `html[data-reduced-motion="true"]` (set by `PreferencesProvider` from the LSI-044
  preference) applies the same neutralization in-app.

The manual checklist (§6) verifies the OS setting and the in-app preference both take
effect on the named surfaces.

### 3.3 Token / primitive compliance

`src/components/ui/__tests__/design-contract.test.ts` (32 assertions) and
`design-tokens.test.ts` (7 assertions) already enforce semantic tokens, the standard
type/spacing scale, and no hardcoded palette/literal colors across every
`components/ui` primitive; they pass unchanged in the full suite.

### 6.2 What to verify on every row

1. **No overflow:** no horizontal scrollbar or clipped controls at 390px and at the
   short viewport; the primary action stays reachable.
2. **No trapped controls:** Tab/Shift+Tab can enter and leave every region; Escape
   closes dialogs/drawers/menus and returns focus to the trigger; focus is never lost
   off-screen.
3. **No dead actions:** every visible button/link/tab does something observable (no
   `onClick`-less controls, no placeholder toggles).
4. **Visible focus:** the focus ring is visible in both themes.

### 6.3 Journey-specific checks

* **Guest → lesson (rows 1–3):** keyboard-open a catalog card, toggle List⇄Canvas with
  the keyboard, move through lesson prev/next, and open a source link. Confirm the
  "Save copy / track progress / ask the tutor" gates are focusable and lead to
  `/login`.
* **Tutor + branch (rows 6–7):** Tab to the composer (label announced), send a
  message, **select lesson text to quote** (quote banner appears and its clear
  control is reachable), press **Escape** to exit any open menu, and reload — the
  active quote/reading position must be preserved and the branch pane must keep the
  correct root URL. Verify ancestry/sibling tabs are arrow-key navigable.
* **Generator (row 9):** Tab through the goal form, expand "Background and planning
  details", confirm the review snapshot appears with no editable controls, and confirm
  validation errors are announced (`role="alert"`).
* **Files (row 10):** trigger the hidden file input via the **Upload** button
  (keyboard), confirm the search box and category tabs are labeled, and confirm the
  delete `ConfirmDialog` traps focus, closes on Escape, and is announced as an alert
  dialog.
* **Settings (row 11):** arrow-key through the vertical tabs, open the Provider/Model
  selects with the keyboard, toggle the **Reduced motion** and theme controls, and
  confirm **Reset to defaults** opens a focus-trapped confirm dialog.
* **Reduced motion (all rows):** enable OS "Reduce motion" **and** the in-app
  Reduced-motion setting; streams, skeletons, pulse indicators, and open/close
  animations must not move.
